(iv) define graph?
--  it consists of a collection V of nodes
and a collection E of edges, each of which “joins” two of the nodes. We thus represent an edge e ∈ E as a two-element subset of V: e = {u, v} for some u, v ∈ V, where we call u and v the ends of e.
(v) what is a path?
-- we deﬁne a path in an undirected graph G = (V , E) to be a sequence P of nodes v_1, v_2 , . . . , v_(k−1), v_k with the property that each consecutive pair v_i , v_i+1 is joined by an edge in G. P is often called a path from v_1 to v_k , or a v_1 - v_k path.
